Two students, one small project

The makers

The Long Look is a student project built by two BSIT students from Lewis College, Sorsogon City. One builds the backend — the archive pipeline, email delivery, and the reminder store. One builds the frontend — the layout, the design system, and everything you actually see and click.

Luige builds the backend that turns a dropdown selection into a real work in your inbox. He's responsible for the open archive integration, the EmailJS delivery layer, the local reminder store, and the fact that nothing breaks when you schedule a reminder for midnight on a Sunday.

How we split it

Two halves, one loop

Same programme, same campus, two very different halves of the same build. Luige makes it work. Austin makes it worth looking at.

Luige makes it work

  • Queries the open art archives (Cleveland + AIC)
  • Filters out records with missing images
  • Builds and sends the reminder email via EmailJS
  • Stores your study list locally on your device
  • Handles validation, error states, and retries

Austin makes it look right

  • Designs the gallery-style layout and frame system
  • Builds the responsive grid from 360px up
  • Writes the design tokens and hover states
  • Handles the lightbox, dashboard cards, and transitions
  • Keeps every page visually consistent

About the build

A student project with a real purpose

The Long Look started as a coursework project at Lewis College in Sorsogon City. The brief was open — build something on the web that uses a public API and does something genuinely useful with it.

We picked the Art Institute of Chicago's open archive because it's free, keyless, and enormous — over a hundred thousand works, all properly credited. Then we built the smallest possible thing on top of it: a personal reminder that emails you one work, on your schedule, with a materials list and prompts for looking at it properly.

Everything runs in the browser. No accounts, no servers to maintain, no tracking. Just two students, one open archive, and a small idea about paying attention.
